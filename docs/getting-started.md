Download and install TLS Reader, then sign in to load your library.

## 1. Download the app

Open the [latest TLS Reader release](https://github.com/thorrsson/tls-reader/releases/latest) and download the installer for your operating system:

- **macOS:** Open the DMG and move TLS Reader to Applications.
- **Windows:** Download and run the x64 installer.
- **Linux:** Download the x64 AppImage, make it executable if your desktop does not do so automatically, then launch it.

Release assets and operating-system security warnings may vary by version. Only download TLS Reader from the project's official GitHub releases.

## 2. Sign in

Launch TLS Reader and enter the email address and password for your TLS Library account. The app connects to the library service configured for this build; an internet connection is needed to sign in and load your catalog.

You can paste credentials from a password manager using **Edit → Paste**, `Cmd+V` on macOS, or `Ctrl+V` on Windows and Linux. You can also right-click a sign-in field and choose **Paste**. TLS Reader does not read your clipboard through its app bridge or store your password.

## 3. Open your library

After signing in, your available publications appear in the library. Choose a book to open it. Use **Library → Refresh** if you want to reload the catalog.

To sign out, choose **Account → Sign out** from the desktop menu.
